A hover card previews what's behind a link, such as a person's profile
behind their username, for sighted users: it opens after a short delay
while the link is hovered or focused with the keyboard, and stays open
while the pointer is over it. Screen readers don't announce it, so the
link must make sense without it.

## Do

- Use it for a preview of something people can also open.
- Keep it short: a name, a line of text, a date.

## Don't

- Don't put anything only in the card: what it says must be on the page
  it previews.
- Don't put buttons or forms in it; use a Popover.
