# Collapsible

## 0.2.0

Every part of the item is now its own directive without the `Nd` prefix, which is kept for the headless primitives. Renames `NdCollapsible` to `Collapsible`, `NdCollapsibleTrigger` to `CollapsibleTrigger` and `NdCollapsibleContent` to `CollapsibleContent`. Selectors and inputs are unchanged, and `ndop update` renames the imports and their uses in your code.
