## 1.0.0

## 0.1.2

The visitors chart uses the chart's HTML axis labels, which keep their
size on phones and wide screens. Its plot is 224px high at every width,
stretched across the card, instead of a third of its width, so it's
neither flat on a phone nor tall on a desktop. The first and last days
are labelled, with the labels between evenly spaced, and the edge labels
stay within the plot.
